About Desert Wings

Industrial and event catering, packed meals, cafeterias, housekeeping and laundry — delivered from Al Wakra to organisations across Qatar.

Our story

Built to feed and support the people who build Qatar

Desert Wings W.L.L. was designed to provide industrial and event catering services. Over more than a decade, we have become a major supplier of food products and hospitality services to organisations across the region.

Alongside catering we provide housekeeping and laundry services, so clients can hand over the day-to-day welfare of their workforce to one dependable partner. We offer flexible food options for every location and for special-needs diets.

Our primary focus is ensuring a robust system of food safety and hygiene — because every meal we serve is fuel for someone's working day.
